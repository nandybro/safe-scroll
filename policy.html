Privacy Policy – SafeScroll

Effective Date: 22 February 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Year of birth

This information is securely stored in Firebase and is used only for user identification, account management, subscription management, customer support, and basic personalization of the app experience. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes.

Location Information (Optional): If you choose to use our location-based blocking feature, the app may access your device's location. This feature is disabled by default and requires your explicit manual activation and permission.

2. Accessibility Service Permission

SafeScroll uses Accessibility Service strictly to provide core safety features. It is used to:

  • Detect and block adult or explicit content in real time
  • Detect when restricted apps are opened to apply focus limits
  • Automatically exit restricted apps using system navigation actions
  • Monitor app protection settings to prevent unauthorized removal

SafeScroll may take temporary screenshots to analyze content using on-device AI.

Important:

  • Images are processed locally on your device
  • Screenshots are never stored
  • Screen data is never uploaded
  • Screen content is never shared

All content detection runs entirely offline on your device.

3. Location Data & Geofencing (Optional)

SafeScroll provides an optional feature allowing users to block or restrict apps based on physical location. Location tracking is disabled by default.

Key details regarding our location handling:

  • Manual Opt-In: Location access is only granted if you explicitly provide permission and manually turn on location-based blocking inside the app.
  • 100% Offline Processing: Location checks and geofence evaluations are performed entirely offline and locally on your device.
  • No Cloud Storage or Transmission: Location data is never transmitted, synced, or stored in Firebase, cloud databases, or any remote servers.
  • Single Purpose: Location information is used solely to enforce your configured location-based blocking rules. It is never used for user tracking, profiling, analytics, or advertising.
  • Automatic Deletion: Any saved location rules or coordinates remain solely in your device's local application storage and are automatically and permanently deleted when the app is uninstalled or when local app data is cleared.

4. Data Storage & Security

Personal account data (email, phone number, year of birth) is securely stored in Firebase. Screen data, AI detection results, and location data are processed strictly locally on your device and are never stored on external servers or cloud systems. All local data is automatically purged upon app uninstallation. We implement reasonable administrative and technical security measures to protect your information.

5. Children’s Privacy

SafeScroll may be installed by parents on devices used by children. We do not knowingly collect additional personal data from children beyond account information provided by the parent or guardian. Parents are responsible for supervising their child’s device usage.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information only with secure service providers (such as Firebase for account authentication and subscription handling) or if required by law. Local data (including location and screen analysis) is never shared.

7. Your Control

You have full control over your permissions and data at all times:

  • You can grant, deny, or revoke permissions (such as Location or Accessibility) at any time via your device settings.
  • You can enable or disable location-based blocking directly inside the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account data or uninstall the app at any time, which automatically purges all locally stored data.

Email: contact@safescroll.app
