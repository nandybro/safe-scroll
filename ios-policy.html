Privacy Policy – SafeScroll (iOS)

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

SafeScroll for iOS is built from the ground up with your privacy as our absolute priority. No information is transmitted to our servers or stored locally on your device. We do not collect personal information, usage metrics, or any other data.

2. Screen Recording & Content Processing

In order to provide its core functionality, SafeScroll uses the iOS Screen Recording capability to identify whether adult content is present on your screen.

  • Processed in RAM: The screen recording data is entirely processed in the device's Random Access Memory (RAM).
  • No Storage: No screen data, images, or frames are ever saved, cached, or stored on your device's disk.
  • No Transmission: Screen data is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted over the internet to us or any third party.
  • Instant Deletion: The screen data is immediately discarded from RAM once the real-time processing is complete.

3. Data Deletion

Because we do not store any data within the app or on any external servers, there is no personal data to delete. If you choose to uninstall the SafeScroll app, all associated app files and settings are permanently removed by iOS.

4. Your Control

You retain full control over SafeScroll's permissions. You can stop the Screen Recording capability at any time through the standard iOS interface or revoke the app's permissions in your device settings. You can also uninstall the app at any time without leaving any traces of data.
